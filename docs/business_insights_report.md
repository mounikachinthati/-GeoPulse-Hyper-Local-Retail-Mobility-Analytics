# GeoPulse – Business Insights Report

## 1. Overview

This report presents business insights from 200 mapped locations in the selected study area of Guntur, Andhra Pradesh.

## 2. Retail Distribution

| Metric | Count |
|---|---:|
| Total locations | 200 |
| Retail locations | 62 |
| Non-retail locations | 138 |

- Retail percentage: 31%
- Non-retail percentage: 69%

## 4. Key Business Insights

1. Fashion and apparel is the largest identified retail category, with 17 locations.
2. Shopping is the second-largest category, with 10 locations.
3. Restaurants account for 7 locations.
4. Retail businesses represent 31% of all mapped locations.
5. The dataset covers a limited geographic area, with latitude values from 16.288858 to 16.294196 and longitude values from 80.446676 to 80.461184.

## 5. Business Opportunities

- Retailers can study areas with fewer mapped businesses before considering expansion.
- New businesses can investigate customer demand in categories with relatively few mapped locations.
- Existing retailers can use the interactive map to understand nearby business distribution.
- Location-based planning can help businesses compare potential sites.

These are opportunities for further investigation, not confirmed market gaps. Customer demand, competition, rent and purchasing power must be studied before making investment decisions.

## 6. Recommendations

1. Collect customer footfall data.
2. Gather sales information from local retail businesses.
3. Expand the geographic coverage of the dataset.
4. Validate business categories and locations.
5. Combine sales and location data for stronger business decisions.

## 7. Limitations

- Only 200 locations from a selected area are analyzed.
- Retail classification uses predefined category rules.
- Actual customer footfall and sales data are unavailable.
- The analysis does not establish customer demand or profitability.

## 8. Conclusion

The analysis identifies 62 retail locations among 200 mapped places. Fashion and apparel is the leading identified retail category. These findings provide an initial foundation for location-based retail planning in Guntur.
