# GeoPulse: Hyper-Local Retail Mobility Analytics

## 1. Problem Statement

Retail businesses need to understand the geographic distribution of nearby shops, customer activity and local market patterns to make better business decisions.

However, information about retail locations, customer footfall and sales is often scattered across different sources.

GeoPulse aims to develop a hyper-local retail analytics platform that combines geographic business-location data with mobility and retail activity data to identify retail hotspots, analyze local competition and generate useful business insights.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Identify retail shops and business locations in selected geographic areas.
- Analyze the geographic distribution of retail businesses.
- Identify high-density retail areas and potential market opportunities.
- Study customer footfall patterns using available or clearly labelled synthetic data.
- Analyze retail sales trends using available or clearly labelled synthetic data.
- Build interactive dashboards and visual reports.
- Generate actionable insights for retail planning.

## 3. Project Scope

The initial study focuses on Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India.

The project uses Overture Maps Places data to identify local business locations. Additional footfall and sales datasets will be integrated during development.

## 4. Expected Outcomes

- Cleaned retail location dataset.
- Geographic retail hotspot analysis.
- Retail category and competition analysis.
- Footfall and sales trend analysis.
- Interactive analytics dashboard.
- Project documentation and final presentation.

## 5. Technology Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- SQLite
- Streamlit
- Plotly
- GeoJSON
- FastAPI

## 6. Data Sources

- Overture Maps Places dataset for business locations and geographic information.
- Additional public datasets or clearly labelled synthetic datasets for footfall and retail sales analysis.

## 7. Data Limitations

Mapped business locations do not directly represent customer visits or actual sales. Footfall and sales insights will only be presented when supported by real data or explicitly labelled synthetic datasets.
